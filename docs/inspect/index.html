    Where every value comes from.

    The invoices are fictional (Qingmar Steel Co. does not exist) and the money is test USDC. The outputs are not: each one was produced by the code in the repository, and the links let you check it.

    • The scanThe reader's own result on the PDFs in reader/corpus/: fingerprint, hidden text dropped with its reason, the verdict before any AI call. Re-run it with tools/read_invoice.py --dry-run.
    • The AI's answerThe live reading of the honest invoice on NEAR AI Cloud, saved whole in docs/proof/reading-INV-2208.json: request, response, signature and attestation report. It re-checks offline.
    • The contract's checksThe reason codes are the contract's own (src/inspection.rs). Two verdicts are live receipts on guard.cleartopay.testnet; the honest invoice's first payment runs after the supplier's 72-hour wait; the one-signer case is a contract test.
    • What happens nextThe follow-up text is what tools/next_step.py printed for the live receipts: facts from the signed records, a draft for a person to read, or "no draft" for a seizure.
