How it reads

Seven calls. All read-only.

Each section of this page is one view method on the contract, called over NEAR's public JSON-RPC at final finality.

  1. receipt_count · get_receiptEvery verdict with its reasons, newest first.
  2. get_paymentFor cleared invoices: the payment state and the attempt number.
  3. get_fundsUSDC available and reserved in the pool.
  4. get_in_flightTransfers sent and not yet confirmed. Their money stays reserved.
  5. get_limitsThe caps the agent cannot exceed.
  6. get_supplierThe one address money may go to, and when it became payable.
