      AlertsEveryone hears it on Telegram

      Each person taps Start once. The buyer hears everything; a supplier hears about its own payout address (while a change is still in its 72-hour wait), its invoices and its payments; delivery signers hear when a signer is added. Only public on-chain facts are sent.

      Advanced One change at a time: fixes, the pool, the agent's key, single invitations

      InviteBring people in with one link

      Send a supplier or a delivery signer a link. They open it on their phone, a key is made there, and their details come back to you as a link that fills in the right form below. You still review and sign every registration, and you still confirm a supplier's payout account by phone.
