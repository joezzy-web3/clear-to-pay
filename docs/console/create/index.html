Step 2 · approval 1Create the contract

One ordinary call to our factory with 3.6 NEAR attached. In one all-or-nothing step it creates your contract's account, puts your 3.6 NEAR on it for storage, installs the contract and makes you its owner. No access key is added, so the account is locked. If anything fails, nothing is created and your 3.6 NEAR comes back. Then this page reads the contract back from NEAR itself.
